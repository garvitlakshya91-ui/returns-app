FAQ

Questions merchants ask us

Do I need a Royal Mail or Evri business account?

No — that's the point of managed labels. Turn them on with one click and ReturnFlow generates real labels through our carrier network. Each label is billed at cost + a small fee on your normal Shopify invoice. If you already have your own Royal Mail Click & Drop account, you can connect it instead.

How long does setup take?

About two minutes: install, enable managed labels, set your return address, and share your portal link. Your return window and policies are configurable but come with sensible UK defaults (30-day window).

Can customers get store credit or exchanges instead of refunds?

Yes. You choose which resolutions to offer — refund to original payment, store credit (issued as a Shopify gift card), or an exchange for a different size/colour. You can offer different options per product or reason.

Does it work with my theme?

The returns portal is a standalone page (yourstore's own branded link) so it works with every theme, no code changes. You can set your logo, colours and heading in Settings.

What happens if I go over my plan's return limit?

Nothing breaks — customers can still submit returns. We'll prompt you to upgrade, and you can move between plans (or back to Free) anytime, self-serve.
